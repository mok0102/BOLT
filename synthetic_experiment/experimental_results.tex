\subsection{Synthetic Branin Results}
\label{sec:synthetic-results}

Figure~\ref{fig:synthetic-results} compares STBO, BOLT, and the
available one-step ORPT run ($H=1$) across training milestones. Each point is the mean over the same 20 held-out tasks;
the error bars in the regret figure show standard errors. STBO has no
milestone-dependent training stage, so its curve is constant.

\paragraph{Initialization quality.}
The learned initializers substantially improve the best score in the
five-point initialization pool relative to STBO. At milestone 50, the mean
best initialization scores are $-11.607$ for STBO, $-4.781$ for BOLT,
and $-4.754$ for ORPT. ORPT generally yields a better initialization than
BOLT from milestone 5 onward, with a small reversal at milestone 10. The
initialization-score figure uses a broken vertical axis to show both the
large STBO gap and the smaller BOLT--ORPT differences without altering the
underlying values.

\paragraph{Outcome after BO.}
After 50 additional oracle calls, all three methods approach the mean
optimal score of $-4.750$ for this held-out task set. At milestone 50,
ORPT reaches a mean best score of $-4.7537$, compared with $-4.7578$ for
BOLT and $-4.7645$ for STBO. The corresponding mean simple regrets are
$0.0037$, $0.0078$, and $0.0145$, respectively. ORPT has lower mean final
regret than BOLT at every milestone from 5 through 50; at milestone 2,
the two methods are nearly tied and BOLT is slightly lower. These trends
suggest that improving the initial pool can continue to benefit the
subsequent BO trajectory, even when all methods use the same 50-call BO
budget. The figure shows descriptive means and standard errors; no
statistical significance claim is made from these plots alone.
